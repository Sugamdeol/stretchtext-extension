How it works

  1. Hover any paragraph on a page.
  2. A small control appears next to it.
  3. Click Expand to grow that text in place with more detail, or Shrink to condense it back.
  4. Keyboard: Alt + E expands, Alt + S shrinks.

Nothing is rewritten until you click. The original wording is kept, so Restore always brings the page back.
